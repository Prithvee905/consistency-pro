Made for a student's week.

Weekly goals, not daily
Set gym for four days a week. The other three are rest days and nothing breaks.
A bad-day version
Can't do 45 minutes? Do 10 and it still counts. Ticking a task can log it for you.
Alarms for your timetable
Pick the days and the time. Classes, study blocks and gym slots repeat, and the alarm rings when they start.
Graphs of your own progress
A bar chart for the last seven days and a calendar for the last sixteen weeks, for every habit.
Your data stays on your phone
Habits, tasks and logs are saved on your device. A public page exists only if you choose to create one.

Your streak, sized for your story.

Open any habit, tap share, and pick a card. It exports at 1080 by 1920, the size Instagram stories use.

One option is see-through, so you can place your streak over your own gym or desk photo.

Want a link for your bio? Create a public page that shows your habit names, streaks and the days you logged. Nothing else.
